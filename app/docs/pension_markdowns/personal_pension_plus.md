## Product Overview

HDFC Life Personal Pension Plus is a non-linked participating deferred pension plan designed to help customers build a retirement corpus while participating in the profits of the participating fund through bonuses.

The product provides:
- assured vesting benefits
- participating bonuses
- death protection
- long-term retirement accumulation
- flexible vesting age selection

The plan is suitable for customers seeking a combination of:
- guaranteed retirement benefits
- long-term accumulation
- bonus participation
- retirement income planning

## Vesting Benefit

On survival until vesting date and full payment of premiums, customer receives higher of:

- Sum Assured on Vesting plus accrued bonuses
- 101% of total premiums paid

### Vesting Utilization

Benefits governed by pension regulations and may require:
- annuitization
- partial commutation
- pension conversion

---

## Death Benefit

On death of the life assured:

Nominee receives:
- 101% of total premiums paid
- accrued bonuses (if declared)

### Minimum Death Benefit

Minimum death benefit:
- 105% of total premiums paid

### Nominee Options

Nominee may:
- purchase immediate annuity
- withdraw lump sum benefit

---

## Bonus Structure

### Reversionary Bonus

- Declared as percentage of Sum Assured on Vesting
- Once declared, becomes guaranteed
- Payable at vesting

Depends on:
- investment returns
- mortality experience
- taxation
- company surplus

---

### Interim Bonus

May apply if:
- vesting occurs before next bonus declaration

---

### Terminal Bonus

- Non-guaranteed
- Based on long-term fund performance
- Payable at vesting if declared

---

## Grace Period Rules

| Frequency | Grace Period |
|---|---|
| Monthly | 15 days |
| Others | 30 days |

During grace period:
- policy remains active
- risk cover continues

---

## Lapsation Rules

Policy lapses if:
- premium unpaid during grace period
- surrender value not acquired

After lapse:
- risk cover ceases
- benefits not payable

---

## Paid-Up Rules

If:
- surrender value acquired
- premiums discontinued

Then policy becomes paid-up.

### Paid-Up Benefits

Paid-up value includes:
- reduced paid-up sum assured
- accrued bonuses

No future bonuses accrue after paid-up conversion.

---

## Revival Rules

Policy revival allowed:
- within 5 years from first unpaid premium

Requirements:
- outstanding premium payment
- revival interest
- underwriting approval

### Revival Interest

Current revival interest:
- 9.5% p.a.

### Revival Processing Fee

- ₹250 processing charge

---

## Surrender Rules

Policy acquires Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV):
- after payment of first 2 years premiums

### Surrender Components

Surrender value includes:
- percentage of premiums paid
- surrender value of bonuses
