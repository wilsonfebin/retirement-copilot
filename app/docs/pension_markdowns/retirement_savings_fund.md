## Product Overview

HDFC Retirement Savings Fund – Equity Plan is an open-ended retirement solution-oriented mutual fund scheme designed to help customers build long-term retirement wealth through equity market participation.

The scheme combines:
- retirement-oriented investing
- long-term wealth creation
- equity market exposure
- systematic investment planning
- tax-saving benefits

The scheme includes a mandatory lock-in period and is intended for long-term retirement accumulation.
