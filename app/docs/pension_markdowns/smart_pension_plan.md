## Product Overview

HDFC Life Smart Pension Plan is an individual, unit-linked, non-participating pension plan designed to help customers accumulate retirement wealth through market-linked investments while providing life insurance protection.

The product supports:
- retirement corpus accumulation
- long-term wealth growth
- pension-focused investment planning
- multiple investment fund options
- annuity conversion at vesting
- automatic investment strategies

The plan combines:
- equity exposure
- retirement planning
- insurance protection
- long-term market participation

## Vesting Benefit

On survival until vesting:
- customer receives fund value

### Vesting Utilization Options

Customer may:
- purchase annuity
- partially commute pension corpus
- defer annuity purchase subject to regulations

### Commutation Rules

Up to:
- 60% commutation permitted subject to prevailing regulations

---

## Death Benefit

On death before vesting:
nominee receives higher of:
- fund value
- 105% of total premiums paid

### Nominee Options

Nominee may:
- withdraw death benefit
- purchase immediate annuity
- purchase deferred annuity

## Investment Strategies

### Automatic Asset Rebalancing Strategy

Automatically reduces equity exposure as vesting approaches.

Purpose:
- reduce market volatility risk
- preserve retirement corpus

---

### Systematic Transfer Strategy

Gradually transfers investments:
- from debt-oriented funds
- into equity-oriented funds

Supports:
- rupee-cost averaging
- phased market exposure

Applicable primarily for:
- annual premium policies
