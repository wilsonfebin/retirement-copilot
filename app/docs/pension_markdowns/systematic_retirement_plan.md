## Product Overview

HDFC Life Systematic Retirement Plan is an Individual/Group, Non-Participating, Non-Linked, Savings Deferred Annuity Plan designed to help customers systematically build a retirement corpus and receive guaranteed lifelong income after retirement.

The plan allows customers to:
- defer annuity payouts
- select premium payment terms
- choose annuity payout frequencies
- select annuity options
- receive guaranteed lifelong income

The annuity rate is guaranteed at policy inception and remains fixed throughout the policy duration.

## Annuity Options

### 1. Life Annuity

- Guaranteed annuity for lifetime
- No death benefit after deferment period
- Policy terminates on death of annuitant

---

### 2. Life Annuity with Return of Premiums

- Guaranteed annuity for lifetime
- Return of premiums benefit on death
- Death benefit payable to nominee/legal heirs

## Annuity Payout Frequencies

Customers can receive annuity payouts in:

- Quarterly

- Yearly

## Deferment Rules

### Deferment Period

The deferment period:
- starts from policy inception
- determines when annuity payouts begin
- can differ from premium payment term

### Payout Timing

| Frequency | First Payout Timing |
|---|---|
| Yearly | 1 year after deferment |
| Half-Yearly | 6 months after deferment |
| Quarterly | 3 months after deferment |
| Monthly | 1 month after deferment |

---

## Survival Benefits

### During Deferment Period

- No survival benefits payable

### After Deferment Period

- Guaranteed annuity payouts payable for lifetime
- Payouts continue as long as annuitant survives

---

## Death Benefits

### Death During Deferment Period

Death benefit payable is higher of:

- Total premiums paid accumulated at 6% compounding interest
- 105% of total premiums paid

Policy terminates after payout.

---

### Death After Deferment Period

#### Life Annuity Option

- No death benefit payable

#### Life Annuity with Return of Premiums

Higher of:
- accumulated premiums less annuity payouts
- 105% of total premiums paid

## Grace Period Rules

---

## Lapse Rules

Policy lapses if:
- first full-year premium not paid
- grace period expires

After lapse:
- no benefits payable
- all benefits cease

---

## Reduced Paid-Up Rules

If:
- at least one full-year premium paid
- subsequent premiums unpaid

Then:
- policy becomes Reduced Paid-Up

### Paid-Up Formula

```text
Paid-up Annuity Rate =
Annuity Rate ×
(Total Premiums Paid / Total Premiums Payable)
```

---

## Revival Rules

Policy revival allowed:
- within 5 years from first unpaid premium
- subject to underwriting approval

### Revival Interest

Current revival interest:
- 9.5% p.a. compounded

Interest reset based on:
- 10-year G-Sec yields
- IRDAI approval

---

## Surrender Rules
